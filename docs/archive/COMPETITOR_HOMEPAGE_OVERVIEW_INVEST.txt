EXECUTIVE SUMMARY — PATTERNS ACROSS ALL 12
================================================================================

1. ROLE / AUDIENCE GATE IS STANDARD
   Almost every pure asset manager forces you to declare who you are before content:
   Personal / Individual | Financial adviser | Institutional | Corporate.
   Stanlib, M&G, Ashburton, Coronation, Ninety One all use this pattern.

2. THREE HOMEPAGE ARCHETYPES
   A) PURE ASSET MANAGER (fund + philosophy led):
      Allan Gray, Coronation, Ninety One, Stanlib, M&G, Prescient, Ashburton.
   B) LIFE-STAGE / GOAL JOURNEY (retail-friendly):
      Sygnia, Old Mutual Invest, Coronation (guided journeys), Allan Gray (What we offer).
   C) GROUP / DISTRIBUTION HUB (not a single retail invest homepage):
      Sanlam Investments (multi-business + B2B), PSG (wealth group + advisers),
      Momentum (full insurer/medical aid/estate stack).

3. HERO MESSAGING ANGLES
   - Philosophy & time horizon: Allan Gray ("Building long-term wealth"), Coronation ("long-term thinkers").
   - Democratisation / access: Sygnia ("Money Management for All"), Prescient ("certainty in uncertain world").
   - Change / global scale: Ninety One ("Investing for a world of change", R3.9tn AUM stats).
   - Track record proof: Allan Gray (R10k since 1998 chart), Coronation (#1 fund rankings).
   - Campaign carousel: Ashburton, Sanlam Investments, Ninety One (insights-led slides).

4. EDUCATION IS PROMINENT (unlike insurers)
   New-to-investing journeys, calculators, InvestRight/Fund Selector, Corospondent/Taking Stock
   publications, video series, retirement guides. This is where AS Brokers /home3 aligns well.

5. ADVISER VS DIRECT
   - Direct digital invest: Coronation (Invest Now), Old Mutual Invest (online application), M&G (online forms).
   - Adviser-intermediated: Allan Gray ("Find an independent financial adviser"), PSG (1,000 advisers),
     most others offer both paths.
   - AS Brokers sits firmly in adviser-led camp — similar to Allan Gray / PSG, not OUTsurance-style direct.

6. RETIREMENT IS EVERYWHERE BUT RARELY THE HERO QUESTION
   Retirement content exists (RA guides, two-pot hubs, "on track to retire" articles) but few lead with
   "Will I have enough?" as the primary homepage question. Sygnia life-stage cards and Ninety One
   retirement carousel slide are closest. AS Brokers /home3 retirement funnel is differentiated.

TAKEAWAYS FOR AS BROKERS (INVESTMENT / RETIREMENT POSITIONING)
================================================================================

WHAT WORKS (worth borrowing for /home3 and retirement content)
  • Sygnia life-stage cards ("What are you saving for?") — plain language, retirement explicit
  • Coronation guided journeys (unit trust / tax-free / RA) — beginner-friendly paths
  • Old Mutual Invest goals nav + InvestRight calculator — goal → amount needed
  • Allan Gray goal-based "What we offer" mega-menu — retirement, TFSA, education, offshore
  • Ninety One retirement carousel ("on track to retire comfortably?") — question as hook
  • Stanlib objective-based fund grouping (Income / Stable Growth / Growth / Offshore)
  • M&G / Coronation calculators — interactive proof before advice conversation

WHAT TO AVOID (asset-manager traps for a boutique adviser)
  • Group holding pages as homepage (Sanlam Investments, PSG) — too much corporate noise
  • Fund catalogue in hero before visitor self-identifies (Ashburton, Prescient news flood)
  • Pure track-record bragging without education (Allan Gray is elegant but intimidating)
  • Insurance-first homepage when selling Everest retirement (Momentum)
