Required product warnings

This is an educational product illustration only. It is not a quote, guarantee, recommendation or offer to invest. Targeted returns, fees, liquidity, insurer acceptance and suitability must be confirmed against the latest approved Amethyst disclosure before advice or implementation.

Returns are not guaranteed. Past or targeted performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Capital values may rise or fall. Concentration risk may apply where portfolio exposure is not broadly diversified across traditional listed markets.

Living annuity drawdowns must remain between 2.5% and 17.5% per year. Growth inside the annuity is generally tax-sheltered; income drawn is taxed at your marginal rate. Compulsory retirement capital and Section 14 transfer rules apply.

Do not use voluntary-product assumptions (for example 12.8% Strategic Income, 14.2% Onyx or 14.5% Growth) inside this illustration. Use only current Amethyst product disclosure.
