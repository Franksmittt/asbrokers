EXECUTIVE SUMMARY — PATTERNS ACROSS ALL 12
================================================================================

1. AUDIENCE SPLIT FIRST
   Almost every large player splits the header by WHO you are before WHAT you need:
   Personal / Business / Corporate (Old Mutual, Sanlam, Absa, Discovery INDIVIDUALS|BUSINESS).
   Santam splits by insurance segment: for me | for my business | specialised risks | agriculture.

2. PRODUCT-LED vs JOURNEY-LED
   - Product-led: Discovery, OUTsurance (quote-first), Liberty (product grids).
   - Journey-led: Sanlam ("I want to: start / saving and investing"), Old Mutual (goal cards:
     Save & invest | Insure | Bank & Borrow), Absa ("What would you like to do?").
   - Corporate-only: TIH, Centriq, Guardrisk (B2B / cell captive / group).

3. HERO = ACTION OR CAMPAIGN
   Dominant hero patterns:
   - Quote / lead form in hero (OUTsurance, Santam call-me-back).
   - Rotating promotional carousel (Old Mutual, Absa, Discovery, Momentum Group).
   - Self-segmentation prompt (Sanlam "I want to:", Hollard three big buttons).

4. LOGIN / PORTAL PROMINENCE
   All retail sites surface client login, adviser finders, or broker portals in header utility row.

5. EDUCATION & TRUST LOWER ON PAGE
   Articles, research, fraud alerts, awards, and "how we work" typically sit mid-to-lower page —
   rarely in primary nav except Discovery (dense product mega-menus).

6. IMPLICATION FOR AS BROKERS
   Your /home2 and /home3 direction (problem-first, education before advice) is NOT how most
   incumbents structure homepages. They assume brand recognition and push products/campaigns.
   AS Brokers can differentiate with journey cards + calculators + education funnel — similar
   to Sanlam's "I want to" and Old Mutual's goal tiles, but deeper and retirement-weighted.

TAKEAWAYS FOR AS BROKERS HOMEPAGE STRATEGY
================================================================================

WHAT WORKS (worth borrowing structurally)
  • Sanlam "I want to:" + calculator row — self-segmentation before products
  • Old Mutual / Absa goal cards — Save | Insure | Bank | Borrow
  • Hollard three plain-language journeys — insure life / invest / business
  • Santam audience-first tiles — for me vs for my business
  • Liberty adviser CTA persistent — "Talk to an adviser" / "Find an adviser"
  • Discovery price anchors on quote tiles — "from R Xm" (transparency)

WHAT TO AVOID (incumbent traps)
  • Campaign carousel as hero (Old Mutual, Absa, Discovery) — pushes promos not clarity
  • Corporate holding page masquerading as consumer entry (Momentum Metro, TIH)
  • Product catalogue in hero before visitor self-identifies (Liberty)
  • Full quote form above fold (OUTsurance) — high friction for education-first brand

AS BROKERS DIFFERENTIATION (aligned with /home2 and /home3)
  • Lead with QUESTION not brand ("What do you need help with today?")
  • Retirement + "not enough money" funnel — none of the 12 do this prominently
  • Calculators inside journey (Sanlam closest with financial check / retirement calc)
  • Education → calculator → article → advice flow — unique vs product mega-menus
  • Independent adviser positioning vs direct insurer or bank-owned distribution
  • No login/portal clutter in header for prospects (all majors surface login early)

RECOMMENDED BENCHMARKS FOR AS BROKERS
  Primary:   Sanlam (journey + tools), Hollard (three journeys), Absa (goal cards)
  Secondary: Old Mutual (life-stage messaging), Santam (audience split)
  Avoid:     OUTsurance hero form, Discovery promo density, corporate group sites
