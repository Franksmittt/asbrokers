Assumptions & disclaimer

This educational projection uses updateable South African estate parameters currently set to: Section 4A abatement R3.5 million; estate duty 20% up to R30 million dutiable and 25% thereafter; executor's remuneration 3.5% of estate value plus 15% VAT.

It assumes annual transfers are made consistently within the selected illustration band, that transferred amounts leave the estate, and that gifted capital may be invested at the growth rate you enter. It does not model attribution rules, donations tax beyond the selected band, trust tax, CGT, loan accounts, inflation separately from growth, changing legislation or your personal legal position.

Results are estimates only and do not constitute legal, tax or financial advice. Confirm every strategy with qualified advisers before acting.
