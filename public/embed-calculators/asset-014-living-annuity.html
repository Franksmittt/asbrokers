What this means for you

Educational note

This calculator illustrates how a living annuity income may look when compared with a targeted annual return. The selected drawdown determines the income paid to you. Any return above the drawdown may help preserve or grow capital, but this depends on product rules, fees, tax, investment performance and annual review terms.

Important living annuity note

A living annuity does not guarantee that your capital will last for life. Drawdown rates are normally selected between 2.5% and 17.5% per year. A higher drawdown creates higher income now but increases long-term sustainability risk.

Product and compliance note

This is an educational illustration only. It is not a quote, guarantee, recommendation or offer to invest. The product provider, investment terms, liquidity rules, return assumptions, fees, tax treatment and suitability must be confirmed before advice or implementation.
