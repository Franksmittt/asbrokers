Educational note

This calculator provides a simplified estimate of South African estate duty, executor's fees and estate liquidity. It uses the current Section 4A abatement (R3.5 million) and estate duty rates of 20% / 25%. It does not include Master's fees, conveyancing costs, valuation costs, capital gains tax, income tax in the estate, funeral expenses or other administration costs. Results are educational estimates only and do not constitute legal, tax or financial advice. Confirm figures during professional legal, tax and financial planning.
