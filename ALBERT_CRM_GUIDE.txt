1. WHAT IS THIS — IN ONE SENTENCE
================================================================================

Your CRM is a bespoke staff command centre on the live website: every enquiry
lands automatically, Gemini AI helps advisors prioritise and respond (FAIS-safe),
and you see the whole pipeline from first contact to won client — built for
AS Brokers services, not a generic off-the-shelf product.

Current maturity: ~8/10 (strong for demos and daily use; portal live data and
full WhatsApp inbox are the next upgrades — see Section 12).

5. HOW LEADS GET INTO THE CRM (AUTOMATIC)
================================================================================

You never manually re-type website enquiries. The system writes to PostgreSQL
(crm_leads table) and shows them in Kanban within seconds.

SOURCES LIVE TODAY
------------------
  CONTACT FORM (/contact)
    Main “Book a consultation” form. Creates lead in “New”. You get email alert.

  NEWSLETTER (footer sign-up)
    Logged as CRM lead for follow-up.

  PLANNING TOOLS & FUNNELS
    • Healthy Retirement Blueprint™
    • Retirement Survival Blueprint™
    • Legacy Readiness Checklist™
    • Business Risk Review™
    Each saves assessment data AND creates/updates the CRM pipeline.

  WHATSAPP (partial)
    Webhook exists; full two-way CRM inbox is Phase 3.

WHAT EACH LEAD RECORD CONTAINS
------------------------------
  • Name, email, phone, company (if provided)
  • Intent / topics they selected
  • Source funnel (e.g. contact form, blueprint)
  • Pipeline stage (New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal, Won, Lost)
  • Lead score (priority hint)
  • Service category — retirement/Everest, estate, short-term, medical, claims, etc.
  • Recommended advisor (auto-routed — see Section 6)
  • AI priority label & score (after you run AI prioritise or open lead detail)

7. GEMINI AI — HOW IT HELPS YOU (LIVE ON PRODUCTION)
================================================================================

Powered by Google Gemini (gemini-2.5-flash) with AS Brokers rules in every
prompt: FSP 17273, FAIS framing, Everest R100k minimum, Amethyst drawdown
2.5%–17.5%, POPIA, no guaranteed returns. Grounded in your compliance/product

DASHBOARD (/crm)
----------------
  • Gemini AI · Morning brief — daily command summary, top priorities,

    (lead-level AI below always works reliably for demos).

KANBAN (/crm/kanban)
--------------------
  • “AI prioritise pipeline” — one click; Gemini ranks active leads and saves
    priority scores to each card.
  • “AI sort: ON/OFF” — sort columns by AI urgency.
  • Priority badges on cards (e.g. “Schedule meeting demo”).
  • Compliance badges — red “Compliance” when drawdown > 17.5% or Everest
    minimum issues detected.

LEAD DETAIL (/crm/leads/[id]) — YOUR BEST DEMO SCREEN
-----------------------------------------------------

    next best action, FAIS-safe talking points, compliance note.
  • “AI draft WhatsApp reply” — populates the message composer in one click.
  • Pre-meeting brief — “Generate brief” → structured agenda, compliance
    checklist → “Print / PDF” for the consultation folder.
  • Correspondence sentiment — neutral / positive / concerned / urgent + tone hint.
  • Compliance flags — drawdown and Everest minimum warnings on the lead.
  • “Open calculator session” — opens meeting calculators linked to this lead;
    after Amethyst walkthrough, log drawdown % → flags appear on Kanban.

EXECUTIVE (/crm/executive) — ADMIN ONLY
---------------------------------------

  • Gemini executive report — owner narrative, strengths, risks, week focus.
  • AI activity log — POPIA audit trail: who triggered AI, what action, when,

8. COMPLIANCE & RISK FLAGS (AUTOMATIC)
================================================================================

The CRM watches for AS Brokers–specific red flags:

  • Amethyst drawdown above 17.5% (regulatory maximum for living annuities)
  • Everest voluntary products below R100,000 minimum capital
  • Drawdown references detected in funnel/risk text

Flags appear on lead detail and as badges on Kanban cards. Advisor should
document suitability review before proceeding — aligned with FAIS practice.
