THE 17 CALCULATORS
==================

ASSET 001 — AS Brokers Retirement Growth Calculator
Category: Retirement planning
What it does: Estimates the growth rate required to reach a retirement capital target from current savings, contributions, and time horizon.
Best for: Clients still working who ask “Am I on track?” or “What return do I need?”
Site fit: /retirement (planning pathway), retirement insights, pre-retirement blog posts.

ASSET 002 — AS Brokers Retirement Reality Check Calculator
Category: Retirement planning
What it does: Compares desired retirement income against projected capital to show a funding gap or surplus (reality check, not a product quote).
Best for: “Will I have enough?” — the core worry funnel on /retirement.
Site fit: /retirement (assessment step), retirement readiness content, semigration articles.

ASSET 003 — AS Brokers Retirement Premium Calculator
Category: Retirement planning
What it does: Illustrates retirement-funding premiums or contribution levels needed to close a capital shortfall over time.
Best for: Clients who know their gap and want a monthly funding number.
Site fit: Life insurance / risk solutions, adviser sessions after reality check.

ASSET 004 — AS Brokers Life of Capital Calculator
Category: Retirement planning
What it does: Models how long retirement capital may last given drawdown rate, inflation, and growth assumptions.
Best for: Already-retired clients worried about running out of money.
Site fit: /retirement (retired pathway + worry funnel), income-in-retirement topics, Amethyst follow-up.

ASSET 005 — AS Brokers Future Value Calculator
Category: Inflation & tax
What it does: Shows how inflation erodes purchasing power of a lump sum or cost over time (future cost of today’s money).
Best for: Education on inflation before investment or retirement conversations.
Site fit: General insights, wealth education articles, cost-of-living topics.

ASSET 006 — AS Brokers Income Tax Calculator
Category: Inflation & tax
What it does: Estimates South African personal income tax, PAYE, and net pay using SARS bracket logic (2026/27 tables in source).
Best for: Tax-aware retirement income planning and take-home pay clarity.
Site fit: Tax / retirement articles, Amethyst drawdown discussions, adviser prep.

ASSET 007 — AS Brokers Estate Duty & Executor Fee Calculator
Category: Estate
What it does: Estimates estate duty (after abatement), executor fees, and total cash the estate may need at death.
Best for: Estate planning introductions and liquidity conversations.
Site fit: /solutions/estate-planning, legacy content, high-net-worth estate articles.

ASSET 008 — AS Brokers Estate Reduction Strategy Calculator
Category: Estate
What it does: Illustrates how annual donations and structured giving may reduce estate duty over time (R100k/R200k allowances).
Best for: Proactive estate reduction and trust/donation planning discussions.
Site fit: Estate planning solutions, annual donation strategy articles.

ASSET 009 — AS Brokers 14.2% Income Calculator
Category: Everest single product
What it does: Illustrates net monthly and annual income from the Everest 14.2% Onyx-style income profile after dividends tax.
Best for: Clients who want higher income from day one without a five-year bonus wait.
Site fit: /everest-wealth, 14.2% product pages, income-focused blog posts.

ASSET 010 — AS Brokers 12.8% Income Calculator
Category: Everest single product
What it does: Illustrates 12.8% Strategic Income returns including the 10% loyalty bonus at year five, with tax on income and bonus.
Best for: Clients open to lower initial income in exchange for the five-year bonus structure.
Site fit: /everest-wealth, 12.8% product content, comparison articles.

ASSET 011 — AS Brokers 12.8% vs 14.2% Income Comparison Calculator
Category: Everest 12.8 vs 14.2
What it does: Side-by-side comparison of 12.8% (with bonus) vs 14.2% income over a five-year horizon on the same capital.
Best for: “Which income product suits me?” — narrow product choice.
Site fit: Everest Wealth hub, product comparison insights.

ASSET 012 — AS Brokers Strategic Growth Portfolio Calculator
Category: Everest single product
What it does: Projects five-year maturity value for the 14.5% Strategic Growth profile (compound growth, tax on accumulated growth).
Best for: Clients who do not need income and want capital accumulation at maturity.
Site fit: /everest-strategic-growth-145, growth-oriented Everest content.

ASSET 013 — AS Brokers Everest Income vs Growth Comparison Calculator
Category: Everest comparison
What it does: Compares three Everest-style strategies: 12.8% income (+ bonus), 14.2% income, and 14.5% growth maturity — monthly income and total outcome.
Best for: Full strategy selection (income need vs growth vs bonus trade-offs).
Site fit: /everest-wealth main funnel, adviser-led product education.

ASSET 014 — AS Brokers Living Annuity Income Calculator
Category: Living annuity
What it does: Estimates living annuity drawdown income, surplus return, and illustrative five-year inflation protection dividend (Amethyst-style assumptions).
Best for: Retirees with pension / RA / preservation capital considering living annuity drawdown.
Site fit: /everest-amethyst-living-annuity, /retirement retired pathway, post-retirement articles.

ASSET 015 — AS Brokers Average Clause Calculator
Category: Insurance
What it does: Shows how much an insurance claim may be reduced when sum insured is less than actual replacement value (average clause).
Best for: Underinsurance education on home, contents, and commercial assets.
Site fit: /solutions/personal-insurance, /solutions/business-insurance, risk articles.

ASSET 016 — AS Brokers The Power of Growth Calculator
Category: Wealth building
What it does: Projects future value of lump sum plus monthly contributions at a chosen growth rate (multi-currency option).
Best for: General wealth-building and compound-growth education across life stages.
Site fit: Wealth insights, business owner content, young accumulator articles.

ASSET 017 — AS Brokers Personal Goal Growth Calculator
Category: Wealth building
What it does: Sets a personal financial goal, required monthly growth rate, and produces a month-by-month contribution spreadsheet to target date.
Best for: Goal-based planning (“I need R X by date Y”) before product selection.
Site fit: /retirement planning pathway, youth / mid-career insights, adviser discovery calls.
