Educational note

This calculator helps retirees evaluate whether current retirement income may be sustainable. It does not recommend an investment product. Outcomes depend on returns, fees, inflation, tax, sequence of returns and future drawdown choices.

Important living annuity note

South African living annuities generally allow annual drawdowns between 2.5% and 17.5%. A higher drawdown creates higher income now but increases long-term sustainability risk. Capital is not guaranteed to last for life.
