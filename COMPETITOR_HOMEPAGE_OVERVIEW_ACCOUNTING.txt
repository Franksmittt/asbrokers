EXECUTIVE SUMMARY — PATTERNS ACROSS ALL 30
================================================================================

1. THREE SITE ARCHETYPES
   A) GLOBAL NETWORK HUB (Big 4 + international brands):
      PwC, Deloitte, EY, KPMG, BDO, Forvis Mazars, Crowe, Baker Tilly, RSM, Moore.
      Industry mega-menus, insights-led heroes, careers funnels, global country pickers.
   B) MID-TIER NETWORK MEMBER (Grant Thornton, PKF, Nexia, UHY, Nolands, Ecovis):
      Service pillars (Audit / Tax / Advisory), publications/news, office locators,
      network membership badges, SME and entrepreneurial positioning.
   C) REGIONAL / BOUTIQUE PRACTICE:
      Morar, MGI Bass Gordon, MD Accountants, C2M, LPH, BVSA, TDP network, Ngubane,
      Rakoma, GRIPP. Simpler nav, partner-led tone, sector or geography emphasis.

2. HOMEPAGE MESSAGING ANGLES
   - Transformation & complexity: Deloitte ("Connecting the dots"), EY ("Shape the
     future with confidence"), KPMG (agile, human-led, tech-forward).
   - Thought leadership as hero: BDO (sector research reports), PwC (AI ROI, infra
     outlook), Forvis Mazars (C-suite barometer), SNG Grant Thornton (IFRS/ESG packs).
   - Trust + access: PKF ("Driven by your ambition"), Nexia ("People Behind the Numbers"),
     Baker Tilly ("Now, for tomorrow"), RSM ("Empowering you to move forward").
   - Public sector / BEE identity: Nexia (majority black-owned), Ngubane (black-owned
     multidisciplinary), Morar (municipal & AG services).
   - Minimal / gate-only: Nolands (country picker), GRIPP (single-line GRC boutique gate),
     Rakoma (multi-business group hub, not pure accounting).

3. NAVIGATION CONVENTIONS
   - Top utility: Careers | Insights/Publications | Contact | Office finder | Log in
   - Service pillars almost always: Audit & Assurance | Tax | Advisory/Consulting
   - Secondary: Industries/Sectors | Forensics | ESG/Sustainability | B-BBEE | Outsourcing
   - Big 4 add: Deals/M&A, Legal, Technology, Risk, Private/Entrepreneur programmes

4. PRIMARY CTAs
   - Enterprise: "Contact us" | "Request for proposal" | "Subscribe to insights"
   - Mid-tier: "Contact us" | "Find an office" | Newsletter signup | "Explore careers"
   - Boutique: "Speak to a consultant" | WhatsApp/phone in header | "Enter" portal pages

5. URL / DOMAIN WARNINGS (critical for research)
   - moore.co.za → unrelated industrial automation company (NOT Moore Global accounting)
   - bvsa.ltd → 404; live site is bvsa.co.za
   - skxprotiviti.co.za → DNS failure; firm operates at skx.co.za (Protiviti member)
   - ngubane.co.za → connection/500 errors; live site is ngubane.co
   - kreston.co.za → SSL trust failure; live network site is krestonsa.com
   - kpmg.co.za → scrape connection errors; kpmg.com/za/en.html loads
   - rsmza.co.za → SSL failure; rsm.global/southafrica loads
   - tdp.co.za → connection failure; TDP network uses tdpsa.co.za, theronduplessis.com, etc.
   - sng-grantthornton.co.za → redirects to grantthornton.co.za
   - nolands.co.za → redirects to nolands.global
   - morar.co.za → scrape returned valid firm HTML; live fetch intermittently shows
     unrelated parked/spam content (domain integrity risk)
   - ashtons.co.za → "Website Under Construction" placeholder only

STRUCTURAL TAKEAWAYS (FOR POSITIONING ANY PROFESSIONAL SERVICES BRAND)
================================================================================

1. Big 4 homepages sell CONFIDENCE AT SCALE: insights carousels, campaigns, and
   careers funnels outweigh service list readability.

2. Mid-tier winners combine THREE homepage elements:
   (a) clear Audit / Tax / Advisory pillars,
   (b) proof (publications, transparency reports, fraud hotlines, BEE credentials),
   (c) human/contact CTAs ("Driven by your ambition", "People Behind the Numbers").

3. Public sector and BEE narrative is a differentiator for Ngubane, Nexia, Morar,
   SkX: geography + transformation credentials above the fold.

4. Broken, parked, or wrong domains are common in legacy firm lists. Always verify
   live URL before UX benchmarking (Moore, BVSA, Kreston, TDP, Ngubane, SkX).

5. Multi-division groups (Rakoma, Nolands) use homepage as a ROUTER, not a service
   catalogue. Pure accounting firms should not mimic this unless truly diversified.

6. Regional firms (C2M, LPH, MD, BVSA) win on GEOGRAPHIC SEO (suburb/office pages)
   and straight service menus, not thought-leadership carousels.

7. For a warm, premium, human-centric public site (cf. AS Brokers marketing direction),
   the accounting sector gap is clear: most homepages remain cold, report-led, and
   template-driven. Opportunity to lead with client life-stage questions and plain
   language before service taxonomy.
