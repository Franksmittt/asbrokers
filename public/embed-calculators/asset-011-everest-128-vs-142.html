Assumptions & notes

Illustrative comparison of two income strategies using assumed dividends tax and an illustrative five-year loyalty benefit on Option A. Underlying product structures may be described elsewhere as Strategic Income (12.8%) and Onyx Income+ (14.2%); product names are secondary—this tool compares outcomes. It is not a quote, guarantee, recommendation or offer to invest. Confirm product terms, risk, liquidity, fees, return profile and tax treatment before any decision.

Important liquidity note

Some income investments, including unlisted preference share structures, may be illiquid. Withdrawals may be restricted, subject to notice periods, penalties, product rules and market conditions. Liquidity differences can matter as much as yield differences.
