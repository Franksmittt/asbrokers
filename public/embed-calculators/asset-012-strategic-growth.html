Who this calculator is for

Investors with voluntary capital who do not need regular monthly income, are comfortable leaving capital untouched for five years, and want to understand how compounding can grow long-term capital. Not intended for retirement funds such as RAs, pension, provident, preservation funds or living annuities.

Important liquidity note

Growth strategies illustrated here are typically designed as fixed-term investments. Early withdrawals may be restricted. Penalties or reduced returns may apply depending on product rules. Ensure you have sufficient emergency savings before committing capital, and seek financial advice first.

Educational note

This tool illustrates the effect of compound growth over a fixed investment term to support investment decisions. It is not a quote, guarantee, recommendation or offer to invest. Returns, tax treatment, liquidity, suitability, fees and product terms must be confirmed before any investment decision is made.
