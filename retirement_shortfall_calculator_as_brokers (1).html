How the calculator works

  • It first inflates today's required income to retirement age.
  • It then converts that future monthly income into a required capital amount using the selected drawdown rate.
  • It projects the future value of existing savings using the selected investment growth rate.
  • It calculates the capital shortfall.
  • It estimates the starting monthly contribution needed, allowing for annual contribution increases.
