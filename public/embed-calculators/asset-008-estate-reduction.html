Educational note

This calculator illustrates how annual tax-free donations may reduce the value of a future estate. It assumes the annual donations are made consistently and that the estate duty saving is calculated at 20%. It does not account for attribution rules, trust tax, CGT, loan accounts, administration costs, changing legislation or personal estate planning needs. It is an educational planning tool and does not constitute legal, tax or financial advice.
